A free, private fitness tracker for fat loss, strength and healthy habits. No sign-up. No ads. Your data stays on your device.

What you get

Weight and trend

7/14/30-day averages, so one noisy day never misleads you.

Workouts

Push/Pull/Legs plan you can edit, set logging, PRs, 1RM graphs, rest timer.

Nutrition

Indian food database, custom foods and recipes, protein and fiber targets.

Steps, water, recovery

Daily goals, streaks and a simple readiness score.

Smart checks

Plateau check that looks at logging, steps and protein before suggesting any change.

Works offline

Install it on your phone like an app. PIN lock, dark and light mode.

Install on your phone

Android (Chrome): open the app, tap the menu (three dots), then Install app.

iPhone (Safari): open the app, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Privacy

Everything you log is stored only in your browser on your device. There is no account and no server. Voice input and the optional AI photo scan send audio or photos to third-party services only when you use them. Export a backup from Settings now and then, because clearing browser data erases your logs.

Please note

This app is for general fitness tracking and is not medical advice. Calorie and body-fat numbers are estimates. Talk to a doctor before starting a diet or exercise plan, especially if you have a health condition.
